## 📱 Mobile App for a Gourmet Sandwich Shop (GSS)

**Role:** UX Designer (end-to-end — conception to delivery)
**Duration:** March 2023 – June 2023

### Overview
Designed a mobile ordering app for a gourmet sandwich shop to help the business attract and retain customers online. The target audience was busy, health-conscious adults (ages 21–80) who order or dine out at least once a week.

### Problem
Working adults and commuters struggle to find time to prepare healthy meals, and existing food ordering options often lack exotic/healthy choices, clear accessibility support, and reliable delivery tracking.

### Process
- **User Research:** Conducted interviews and built empathy maps to uncover user needs and pain points — time constraints, lack of healthy options, accessibility gaps, and delivery delays.
- **Personas & Journey Mapping:** Developed a primary persona (a time-strapped, health-conscious product manager) and mapped their end-to-end journey from app discovery to doorstep delivery, surfacing key improvement opportunities at each step.
- **Wireframing & Prototyping:** Moved from paper wireframes → digital wireframes → low-fidelity prototype → high-fidelity prototype, iterating designs based on usability study findings.
- **Usability Testing:** Identified that users struggled to browse a long, undifferentiated menu list; redesigned the menu with category filters to let users narrow results instead of scrolling a flat list.
- **Accessibility:** Added voice recognition for visually impaired users, icon-based navigation for faster scanning, and support for 5 languages to reduce language barriers.

### Impact
> *"I should say, this app is impressive. I always gotta watch my diet and not always I get to know what I am consuming. The visual representation of dietary info is really useful. I can also choose what I want in my sandwich! I highly recommend this app if you're in search of meals that are exotic yet healthy available at a reasonable price!"* — peer usability feedback
